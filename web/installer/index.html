piegpu installer

Puts piegpu on the Raspberry Pi's microSD card from this page. Step by step, with what to do if something doesn't work: the installation guide.

Supported for now: the Raspberry Pi Zero / Zero W and the Zero 2 W.

This browser can't talk to USB devices: use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer (they have WebUSB and Web Serial). Here you can still prepare a card in this computer's card reader, as a zip (Prepare a card).

1Connect the RPi

A new card: put piegpu on it first. Prepare a card writes it onto the card in this computer's card reader; with no card reader, start the RPi over USB (below). A card with piegpu: connect the RPi's USB port (not PWR IN) to this computer with a data cable, and press Connect (step 2 of the guide).

Put the microSD card in this computer's card reader. It must be FAT32: cards up to 32 GB usually are; a bigger one (exFAT) needs formatting first (how). Choose the board: the Zero 2 W's big chip is marked RP3A0. The settings (section 3) go on the card too. (Way A in the guide.)

Choose the card and write writes the files straight onto the card (Chrome, Edge); Download a .zip gives them to unpack onto the card (any browser). Reset restarts a connected RPi from its card.

No card reader? Start a blank RPi over USB

An RPi whose card is new or empty waits for this computer over USB, and the page starts piegpu on it (a Zero or a Zero 2 W: the board takes its own build); then Install piegpu writes its card. If the card holds another system (Raspberry Pi OS, say), take it out first and put it back when the page asks. Once per computer the browser needs access to the RPi's boot mode: on Linux sudo apt install rpiboot (or a udev rule), on Windows the WinUSB driver (how; Way B in the guide).
