piegpu installer

Puts piegpu on the Raspberry Pi's (RPi's) microSD card from this page, over the RPi's USB port.

Supported for now: the Raspberry Pi Zero / Zero W and the Zero 2 W. More RPi boards are to come.

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer (they have WebUSB and Web Serial).

1Connect the RPi

Put a microSD card in the RPi, then connect the RPi's USB port (not PWR IN) to this computer.

Connect if the card already has piegpu (the panel shows it). Start a blank RPi if the card is new or empty: the RPi then waits for this computer, and the page starts piegpu on it (a Zero or a Zero 2 W: the board takes its own build). If the card holds another system (Raspberry Pi OS, say), take it out first and put it back when the page asks. Reset restarts a connected RPi from its card.
